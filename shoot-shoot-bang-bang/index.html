Privacy Policy

We explain what stays on your phone, which controls you have, and how to delete saved game progress.

1. About this game

Shoot Shoot Bang Bang is a single-player mobile game from SAR67. The release candidate reviewed for this policy runs offline and does not ask you to create an account.

This policy describes the game's own behavior. Your device manufacturer, Apple, Google, and app stores may process information independently under their own privacy notices.

2. What is saved on your device?

The game uses Unity PlayerPrefs to store information locally so you can continue playing:

  • Your unlocked/completed levels and selected level
  • Best squad size and virtual coins
  • Music on/off, sound effects, vibration, and frame-rate preferences

This progress and preference information is stored on your device. The reviewed game code does not send it to any SAR67 server.

3. Your settings and choices

Mute MusicTurns the game's looping background soundtrack on or off. This is a local preference.
Sound EffectsTurns gunfire, game feedback, and other sound effects on or off independently of music.
VibrationControls vibration feedback on devices that support it.
Frame RateLets you choose the frame-rate setting offered by the game, such as 30 or 60 FPS.
These are gameplay preferences, not tracking permissions. The release candidate doesn't offer an advertising or analytics opt-out because it doesn't include those services.

4. Personal information and sharing

Based on the reviewed Unity release project, Shoot Shoot Bang Bang doesn't request a player name, email address, phone number, contacts, photographs, or precise location. It doesn't integrate player chat, analytics, in-game advertising, purchases, or account sign-in. No app feature in the reviewed source uploads saved progress to SAR67.

Data handling by app stores, operating systems, cloud backups, and device diagnostics is outside the game's own code and may follow the privacy policies of those providers. Store privacy declarations will be checked against the final installed build.

5. Retention and deleting data

Local progress and settings remain on your device until you remove them. On Android, you can generally clear the app's storage from device settings. On iPhone or iPad, deleting the app ordinarily removes its local data; offloading an app may keep its data. Device backups may preserve or restore saved information.

There is no SAR67 online player account or SAR67 cloud save to delete in this release.

6. External websites

The game has an optional SAR67 Linktree link. Visiting it opens a separate website; that provider's own privacy policy applies.

This page is hosted on GitHub Pages and does not use any third-party ad scripts, analytics trackers, or data-entry forms. GitHub may handle routine service information in accordance with its privacy statement.

7. Children's privacy

The reviewed game has no social chat, accounts, advertising SDKs, or analytics SDKs and doesn't request personal information from children. Parents and guardians can use device-level controls to manage app access. The appropriate age rating and intended audience will be assessed during store submission based on the game's combat content.

8. Security and updates

No online game-progress server is implemented in this release candidate. Local progress is protected by the device's operating system. Unity PlayerPrefs is not encrypted storage for sensitive secrets. If future versions introduce ads, analytics, accounts, or other personal-data practices, SAR67 will revise this policy and the store privacy disclosures before introducing those features.
