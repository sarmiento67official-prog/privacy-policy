Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 22, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Who Let the Doggos Out, developed and published by SAR67, handles user information.

Information We Collect

Who Let the Doggos Out does not require users to create an account and does not intentionally collect, store, sell, or share personal information such as names, email addresses, phone numbers, precise location, contacts, photos, or payment information.

Device Permissions

The app uses internet access where necessary for basic web functionality and external links. The game does not request access to your camera, microphone, contacts, precise location, SMS messages, or phone calls.

Analytics and Advertising

The current version of Who Let the Doggos Out does not contain third-party advertising SDKs or analytics services.

Local Game Data

The game may use local device or browser-style storage for normal game functionality. Information stored locally remains on the user's device and is not intentionally transmitted to SAR67.

External Links

The game may contain links to third-party websites. Opening these links takes you outside of the app. Those websites may collect information according to their own privacy policies, and SAR67 is not responsible for their privacy practices.

Children's Privacy

Who Let the Doggos Out does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Data Retention and Deletion

Because the app does not intentionally collect or maintain personal user information on SAR67 servers, there is normally no personal account information for SAR67 to retain or delete. Users can remove locally stored game data by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app.

Security

SAR67 takes reasonable steps to minimize the collection of user information and limits the app's permissions to those needed for its functionality.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if the application's features or data practices change. Any updated version will be posted at this same URL with a revised effective date.
